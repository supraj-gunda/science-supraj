02

AtkinsonLab

Recording Plasmid Transfer

  • Designed a CRISPR-based framework to track conjugative plasmid transfer across microbial communities by combining Record-Seq and 16S rRNA barcoding.
  • Tested using MS2 RNA-binding protein/RBS interactions to bias CRISPR spacer acquisition.
  • Applied the SENECA assay to confirm that introducing MS2 RBP did not disrupt spacer acquisition, supporting further integration of RAM and toehold-switch controls.

CRISPR · Synthetic Biology · Microbial Communities

Dynamic Metabolon Formation

  • Developed a reaction–diffusion model of a two-step metabolic pathway with enzyme phase separation.
  • Demonstrated that local product demand can drive enzyme self-organization into dynamic metabolons.

Reaction–Diffusion · Phase Separation · Mathematical Biology
