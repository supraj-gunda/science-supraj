LAB PHOTO CAROUSEL

Replace lab-photo-01.jpg through lab-photo-06.jpg with your own images.
Keep the same filenames and the carousel will update automatically.

Recommended image shape: landscape, about 3:2 (for example 1500 x 1000 px).
The website uses object-fit: cover, so portrait photos also work but may be cropped.

To add or remove slides, edit the “Lab photos and fun” section in index.html.
